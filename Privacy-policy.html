1. Scope

This policy applies to NetAura VPN, including VPN functionality, subscriptions, and ads.

2. Data We Handle

A. VPN Account Data

  • VPN account ID, username, password
  • WireGuard configurations

Used to provide VPN connections. Stored locally and shared with backend providers.

B. Server Data

  • Server IDs, locations, configs
  • Latency measurements

Used for server selection, caching, and recommendations.

C. App Preferences

  • Auto-connect, protocol, selected server
  • Favorites, cached servers
  • Ad frequency controls

Stored locally for app functionality and personalization.

D. Subscription Data

Handled via Google Play Billing. Includes purchase status and entitlements.

E. Ads (AdMob)

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address & approximate location
  • Ad interactions

See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data

F. VPN Traffic

We do not claim to log browsing activity or traffic content unless explicitly implemented and updated here.

3. Permissions

The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. It uses network and VPN permissions only.
