Top Level Developers ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications and a website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with Google Play policies.

1. Information We Collect

Website Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device info
  • Pages visited and usage data
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

Mobile Applications

  • Device information (model, OS)
  • App usage data
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain services
  • Improve performance and features
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Ensure security and prevent abuse

3. VPN Applications

For VPN apps developed by us:

  • We do NOT log browsing activity
  • We do NOT collect traffic or DNS queries
  • We do NOT sell user data

All VPN services operate securely with a strict no-logs policy.

4. AI Applications

  • User inputs are processed only to provide app functionality
  • We do not use personal data for training without consent
  • Data is handled securely

5. Advertising (AdMob)

Our apps may use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Usage data
  • Advertising IDs

This data is used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. Users can control ad personalization via their device settings.

6. Analytics (Firebase)

We may use Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics) to monitor app performance.

  • App usage behavior
  • Crash reports
  • Device information

Firebase processes data according to Google’s privacy policies.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access
  • Network/VPN access (for VPN apps)

Permissions are used strictly for functionality.

8. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect user data, but no system is 100% secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted on this page.
